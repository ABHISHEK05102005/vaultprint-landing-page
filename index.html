How it works

Three steps. No app to install.

Every VaultPrint kiosk runs the same simple flow, designed for the moment before an exam, a submission, or a lecture — when there's no time to lose.

01

Scan the QR

Point your phone camera at the kiosk screen — no app, no login required.

02

Upload your file

PDF, Word, or images — upload straight from your phone in a few taps.

03

Collect your print

Pay on screen and your document prints instantly on A4 paper.
